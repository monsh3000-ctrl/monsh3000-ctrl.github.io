StudyLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

StudyLock helps students convert study activity into banked screen time and optionally block selected apps.

Data we collect

  • Account: Google account name/email via sign-in, to identify your profile.
  • Study content: decks, answers, focus sessions and the screen-time ledger, stored on your device. School materials you import.
  • Files you provide: photos and documents you scan. On-device OCR; if you enable AI generation, extracted text is sent to Google's Gemini API.
  • Google Drive (optional): with your consent, folder and file names you open through StudyLock.
  • School platforms (optional): courses and materials from accounts you connect yourself.
  • Usage: Android usage stats and foreground-app detection to enforce limits you enabled. No screen content is read or stored.
  • Ads: Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID and app interactions per Google's policies.

Permissions explained

Camera (scan notes), usage access (measure screen time), accessibility (detect and block selected apps), notifications (timers and sync), boot (restore timer state).

Storage and sharing

Study data stays on your device. Google services are used only with your consent. We do not sell data.

Contact and deletion

Email: monsh3000@gmail.com. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes on-device data. Revoke Google access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
